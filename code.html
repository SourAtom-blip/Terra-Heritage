Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the process take?

Typically, we present an offer within 48 hours of reviewing your property details. If accepted, closing usually takes 2 to 4 weeks depending on the complexity of title work.

Are there any hidden fees or commissions?

No, there are absolutely no hidden fees. We cover all traditional closing costs. The offer we agree upon is the amount you receive.

What types of land do you buy?

We primarily purchase rural, vacant, agricultural, and recreational land. If you have an unwanted parcel, we are interested in evaluating it.
